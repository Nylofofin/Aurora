# Feedback sounds

The Aurora entry and exit cues are adapted from the `scifi` pack in [UI SFX](https://github.com/romainsimon/uisfx), revision `9950fe66f993a6660dab9c2651dcbcd899ffd83b`.

- `entrada.wav`: `sounds/scifi/wake.ogg`
- `saida.wav`: `sounds/scifi/sleep.ogg`
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication), reproduced in [LICENSE-AUDIO.txt](LICENSE-AUDIO.txt).
- Adaptation: converted to stereo 44.1 kHz PCM WAV and normalized for consistent desktop/mobile playback.

Attribution is not required by CC0, but is retained here for provenance.

## Feedback themes

`entrada-boreal.wav` / `saida-boreal.wav`, `entrada-pulse.wav` /
`saida-pulse.wav` and `entrada-void.wav` / `saida-void.wav` are original
short synthesized cues generated by the Aurora project with Python's standard
`wave` module. They are released under CC0-1.0 and are selectable during
onboarding as Boreal, Pulse and Void.

## Onboarding UI cues

`select.wav`, `step.wav`, `success.wav` and `error.wav` are short synthesized
tones generated by the Aurora project with Python's standard `wave` module.
They contain no sampled or third-party audio and are released under CC0-1.0.
